# SecuryBlack
 
**The monitoring dashboard for freelancers and small teams managing
infrastructure across multiple clients.**
 
🌐 Website: https://securyblack.com

🦀 Open source agent: https://oxipulse.dev
 
---
 
SecuryBlack brings together everything you need to keep client
infrastructure alive in one place:
 
- **Uptime monitoring** for all your client websites
- **Internal server metrics** via OxiPulse, our open source Rust agent
- **Heartbeat checks** for backups and cron jobs
- **Continuous breach scanning** for client emails
 
Built for the freelancer or small agency managing 5–30 client environments
who needs results without assembling a stack of free tools.
 
## Why SecuryBlack
 
Free self-hosted tools work great for one server. They break down at ten.
Maintaining 15 instances of Uptime Kuma, juggling six dashboards, and
finding out from a client that their site went down on Sunday — that's
the problem we built this to fix.
 
→ One account. One dashboard. All your clients.
